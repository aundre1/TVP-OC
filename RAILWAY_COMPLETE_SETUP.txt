CUSTOM DOMAIN SETUP (OPTIONAL)
================================================================================

1. IN RAILWAY DASHBOARD:
   • Go to Deployments tab
   • Click on your deployment
   • Go to Domains tab
   • Click "Add Domain"
   • Enter: staging.thevideopool.com
   • Copy the target CNAME

2. IN YOUR DNS PROVIDER:
   • Create CNAME record
   • Name/Host: staging
   • Value/Points to: [railway-url]
   • TTL: 300
   • Save

3. WAIT & VERIFY:
   • Wait 5-30 minutes
   • Run: nslookup staging.thevideopool.com
   • Visit https://staging.thevideopool.com
   • SSL certificate auto-issued
