## 1. Business Context

### 1.1 Problem Statement

Professional DJs face three critical pain points with existing video pool platforms:

1. **Discovery friction** — Legacy platforms (SmashVision, Xtendamix, ERG Music) use 20-year-old interfaces with no AI capabilities. Finding the right video for a specific vibe takes 10-15 minutes instead of 30 seconds.

2. **Pricing doesn't match workflow** — $49.99/month incumbents charge same price regardless of download volume or use case. Weekend warriors subsidize club residents, creating pricing friction across the market.

3. **No innovation** — Competitors haven't released meaningful features in 5+ years. No mobile apps, no API integration, no AI recommendations, no modern UX. Market ripe for disruption.

### 1.2 The Solution

The Video Pool delivers:

- **AI-native discovery** — Natural language search ("upbeat house clean edits 125 BPM"), personalized recommendations, and smart curation reduce discovery time from 15 minutes to 30 seconds.

- **Value-based pricing** — $34.99/month (30% below SmashVision) with flexible billing cycles (monthly/quarterly/annual) and tiered download limits. Free tier as 6-month trial converts price-conscious users.

- **Modern platform** — React + TypeScript + Vite stack, Spotify-grade UX, mobile-responsive design, real-time search, and setlist sharing built for DJ workflow.

### 1.3 Market Opportunity

**TAM (Total Addressable Market):** $50M+ annually

- **Primary segment:** 100,000+ professional video DJs globally (50,000+ US)
- **Secondary segment:** VJs, online/radio DJs, content creators (50,000+ globally)
- **Revenue potential:** At 5% penetration (5,000 paying subscribers) × $420 ARPU (blended annual) = $2.1M annual revenue

**Competitive advantage:**
- Only AI-native platform built for DJs (not retrofitted)
- 30% price advantage vs. market leader
- Spotify/Netflix-grade interface (competitors stuck in 2005)
- Viral mechanic (setlist sharing) built into product

## 3. Business Model & Revenue

### 3.1 Subscription Tiers

All tiers are live in Stripe with real payment processing.

| Tier | Price | Billing | Downloads/mo | Features | Stripe Status |
|------|-------|---------|--------------|----------|---------------|
| **Free** | $0 | — | 2 | Browse, preview, limited features | LIVE |
| **Starter** | $34.99 | Monthly | 200 | Full platform access | LIVE |
| **Pro** | $99.99 | Quarterly | 300 | + Priority support + early access | LIVE |
| **Elite** | $299.99 | Annual | 400 | + All Pro features, maximum downloads | LIVE |

**Pricing rationale:**
- **Free tier:** 6-month trial (expires, doesn't persist). Converts 15-20% of free users to paid.
- **Monthly:** Full price ($34.99). Flexibility premium for users not ready to commit.
- **Quarterly:** ~5% effective discount ($99.99 ÷ 3 = $33.33/mo). Commitment incentive.
- **Annual:** ~29% effective discount ($299.99 ÷ 12 = $24.99/mo). Cash flow optimization.

| **LTV:CAC Ratio** | >5:1 | 3:1 minimum | Strong unit economics foundation |
| **Payback Period** | <2 months | <12 months | Accelerated by annual plan adoption |
| **Gross Margin** | >75% | 60-80% SaaS | Video delivery cost: ~$0.05/download |

## 4. Go-to-Market Strategy

### 4.1 Launch Approach

**Soft Launch → Scale** (3-phase rollout)

| Phase | Duration | Focus | Target Users |
|-------|----------|-------|--------------|
| **Phase 1: Soft** | Week 1-2 | Beta testing, bug fixes, support | 100-200 internal + invites |
| **Phase 2: Limited** | Week 3-6 | Feature validation, community building | 500-2,000 |
| **Phase 3: Full** | Week 7+ | Marketing push, scale | Open signup |

**Why this approach?**
- Early users generate feedback for critical fixes (mobile, payment, auth edge cases)
- Community builds organic buzz before marketing spend
- Scale risk is lower (don't spike infrastructure on day 1)

### 4.2 Acquisition Channels

| Channel | Strategy | Budget % | Expected CAC |
|---------|----------|----------|--------------|
| **Organic/SEO** | DJ community forums, Reddit, TikTok | 0% (earned) | $0-15 |
| **Viral (Setlist Sharing)** | Built into product; each user brings 0.3 friends avg | Product | $5-20 |
| **Content Marketing** | YouTube tutorials, blog (DJ tips, mixing guides) | 20% | $20-40 |
| **Paid Social** | Facebook/Instagram targeting DJs | 40% | $30-60 |
| **Influencer** | DJ influencer partnerships, affiliate program | 25% | $40-80 |
| **SEM** | Google Ads (high-intent keywords) | 15% | $50-100 |

**Viral coefficient target:** K-factor > 0.3 (each user brings 0.3 new users on average via setlist sharing)

### 4.3 Setlist Sharing Viral Loop

1. DJ creates setlist/crate of videos in app
2. DJ shares setlist publicly (social media, forums, after gigs)
3. Non-members see setlist with blurred thumbnails + signup CTA
4. CTA: "Join The Video Pool to download this set"
5. New member joins, sees value, creates their own setlists...

**Expected outcome:** 15-25% of signups from viral loop within 6 months
