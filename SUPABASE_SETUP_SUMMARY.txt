     - Indexes: 3 (user_id, email, plan)

     - Constraint: UNIQUE (user_id, video_id)

  4. downloads (Download history & analytics)
     - Columns: id, user_id, video_id, downloaded_at
     - Indexes: 3 (user_id, video_id, downloaded_at)

     - Columns: id, user_id, name, description, is_public,
                created_at, updated_at
     - Indexes: 3 (user_id, is_public, created_at)

  6. playlist_videos (Playlist-video many-to-many junction)
     - Columns: id, playlist_id, video_id, position, added_at
     - Indexes: 3 (playlist_id, video_id, position)
     - Constraint: UNIQUE (playlist_id, video_id)
